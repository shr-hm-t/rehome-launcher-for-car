# Rehome: Launcher for Car

<p align="center"><img src="assets/screenshot-home.png" width="49%"> <img src="assets/screenshot-bar.png" width="49%"></p>

Free apps for Android car head units, **made mainly for cars that have a 360 camera**. Tested on one unit only. Made with a lot of help from AI.

**Download both:** [Releases](../../releases)

## Which one do I want?

| | **Rehome: Go** (`Rehome-Go.apk`) | **Rehome 1.7** (`Rehome.apk`) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | A tiny Home app. Pressing Home opens Google Maps. | A full home screen with an offline map and a contacts bar. |
| Best for | People who live in Google Maps, the radio and YouTube and want them one tap away. | People who want a proper home screen, with room for the unit's own 360 picture-in-picture. |
| Extras | A corner frame, a movable pill of buttons (360, Maps, radio/YouTube), a speed light, and **Boot Pack** (makes your own boot logo, animation and wallpaper). | Contacts bar, speed glow, themes. |

You can install both, but only one can be the Home app at a time.

## Install

Download the APK, open it, allow installing from unknown sources. Set the app as your Home app, then restart the unit. **Read [RELEASE_NOTES.md](RELEASE_NOTES.md) first.** It lists what to know before you use them.
